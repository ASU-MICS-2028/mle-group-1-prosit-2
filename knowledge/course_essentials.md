# Course essentials (from the ICS553 syllabus, Fall 2026)

*The parts of the syllabus that affect Prosit 2. Staff contact details are deliberately left out; see the syllabus on the course page.*

## Rules that can cost us marks

- **Late work loses 25% per day.** After two days late the work isn't accepted (0%).
- **AI policy:**
  - **Allowed:** using AI to learn, e.g. clarifying terms and generating practice questions.
  - **Not allowed:**
    - **uploading any provided private dataset into a generative AI tool** (for us, the raw Zindi and Findex files);
    - submitting work substantially generated by AI without critical evaluation;
    - blindly copying AI output.
  - **Required:** declare how AI was used in every assignment. Not declaring counts as an academic honesty violation.
  - **You stay responsible:** you must be able to **explain, defend and reproduce your work without AI**. The live debrief and quizzes exist to check this.
- **Academic honesty:** your contributions must represent your own thinking. Nothing copied from peers, the internet or AI without declaring it.
- **Attendance** at lectures and labs is mandatory.

## Course learning outcomes
1. **LO1** Formulate real-world problems as ML tasks.
2. **LO2** Implement end-to-end ML pipelines.
3. **LO3** Derive core ML algorithms from mathematical principles.
4. **LO4** Evaluate performance with appropriate metrics, diagnostics and statistical validation.
5. **LO5** Design mitigations for bias, fairness and ethical risks.
6. **LO6** Communicate ML to technical and non-technical audiences.
7. **LO7** Critically evaluate ML research papers.

Prosit 2 assesses LO1–LO6; see [`prosit2_brief.md`](prosit2_brief.md#2-learning-outcomes-assessed).

## Readings the syllabus lists
- **Primary:**
  - James et al., *An Introduction to Statistical Learning* (ISLR; free at <https://www.statlearning.com>)
  - Géron, *Hands-On Machine Learning with Scikit-Learn and PyTorch* (HOMLP)
- **Supplementary:**
  - Molnar, *Interpretable Machine Learning* (free at <https://christophm.github.io/interpretable-ml-book/>)
  - Barocas, Hardt & Narayanan, *Fairness and Machine Learning* (free at <https://fairmlbook.org>)
  - Goodfellow et al., *Deep Learning*
- **Must-read papers:** Datasheets for Datasets (Gebru et al. 2021); Model Cards (Mitchell et al. 2019); Gender Shades (Buolamwini & Gebru 2018).
- **Compute:** Kaggle kernels (free GPU, about 30 h/week), Google Colab. Prosit 2 runs fine on a laptop CPU.

## Course format (PBL)
Each prosit runs the same cycle:
1. **Launch:** a problem is introduced.
2. **Initial action plan:** made as a group.
3. **Autonomy:** independent work.
4. **Time with group:** synthesis.
5. **Debrief:** a 15-minute presentation and defence.

Lectures support the work and aren't a substitute for it.
